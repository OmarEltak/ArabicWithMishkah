# Pricing — My-lawyer

> AI-powered contract drafting platform for MENA corporate counsel. Eleven jurisdictions. Bilingual Arabic / English. Citation-verified.

## Free

- **Price:** $0 / month
- **Limits:** 3 contract drafts, 1 user, watermarked Arabic / English export
- **Features:**
  - All 11 MENA jurisdictions
  - All civil-law glossaries (read-only)
  - Citation verification
  - Bilingual side-by-side preview
  - Sample output download (one per template)

## Solo

- **Price:** $39 / month (billed annually) | $49 / month (billed monthly)
- **Limits:** 25 contract drafts / month, 1 user, unlimited revisions per draft
- **Features:**
  - Everything in Free
  - Unwatermarked PDF + Word export
  - Full revision history (10 versions per contract)
  - Glossary customization (per-document overrides)
  - Email support

## Firm

- **Price:** $149 / month (billed annually) | $179 / month (billed monthly)
- **Limits:** 200 contract drafts / month, 5 seats, unlimited revisions
- **Features:**
  - Everything in Solo
  - Shared workspace and contract library
  - Team-wide glossary lock (firm-wide terminology)
  - Citation audit dashboard
  - Comment and review threads
  - Priority support (24-hour response SLA)

## Enterprise

- **Price:** Custom — contact sales@my-lawyer.test
- **Limits:** Unlimited drafts, unlimited seats, custom retention policy
- **Features:**
  - Everything in Firm
  - SSO (SAML / OIDC)
  - Self-hosted deployment option
  - Dedicated multi-key, multi-provider LLM failover
  - Custom glossary onboarding (we ingest your firm's term sheets)
  - Custom jurisdictions outside MENA
  - Dedicated account manager
  - 99.9% uptime SLA

## Notes

- All plans include all 11 MENA jurisdictions: Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan, Lebanon, Tunisia, Libya.
- The legally binding contract is the Arabic version. English is a working translation.
- Citation verification runs on every plan; uncertain citations are flagged before finalization on every plan.
- Annual billing saves ~20%. Monthly is month-to-month with no commitment.
- VAT may apply for EU customers and select MENA jurisdictions.
