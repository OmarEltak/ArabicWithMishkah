# Cold Email Sequence — Cairo Law Firms

> Hand-research 100 Cairo corporate-law firms and partners. Quality > volume.
> Send from the founder's address (e.g. `omar@my-lawyer.app`), not from a
> generic `hello@` — Egyptian lawyers expect a name on the other end.
>
> Cadence: Day 0, Day 4, Day 9, Day 16. Stop on any reply or after 4 touches.

---

## Building the list (1 day of research)

Don't buy a list. Research manually:

1. **Cairo Bar Association** (نقابة المحامين) public directory — filter by year of practice (5–15 yrs = sweet spot)
2. **LinkedIn search**: `"محامي" OR "lawyer" OR "legal counsel"` + location:Cairo + company-size:2-50
3. **Egyptian Law Firms list** (Chambers Global, Legal500 MENA editions)
4. **AUC Law alumni LinkedIn** (every ~3rd alum is a corporate lawyer)
5. **Wamda + MAGNiTT startup directories** → see which lawyers represented recent fundraises in the news (their firms are buyers)

Goal: **100 named lawyers** with verified work email (not Gmail). Spreadsheet
fields: name, firm, focus area, 1 specific signal (recent post, deal, hire).

2. `Saw your post on [topic] — quick question`
3. `[Name], one thing about EG civil-code drafting`
4. `بخصوص الصياغة في مكتبكم` (Arabic — for partners 50+ who prefer Arabic email)

```
[my-lawyer.app/eg]
```

**Rules for email 1:**
- Subject is lower-case, no emojis, no "Re:" tricks
- Specific signal in the first 6 words — proves it's not blasted
- Three numbered points — easy to scan in 8 seconds
- One ask — 15-min slot OR a 90-sec video
- Signature: name only, no title, no banner image (looks like a human)

## What NOT to do (lessons from peer founders)

- **Don't** call the lawyer's office unannounced. Bar Association culture is
  formal — cold-calling is rude. Cold email is fine.
- **Don't** use a CRM-styled signature with social icons. Looks like a sales
  blast.
- **Don't** attach a deck on email 1. Link only.
- **Don't** mention "AI" in the subject line — it triggers spam folder and
  skepticism. Mention it in the body where you control context.
- **Don't** address as "Dear Sir" or "Greetings" — use first name. Egyptians
  in this age bracket find "Sir" stiff/foreign.
- **Don't** send Friday or Saturday (Egypt weekend). Mon/Tue/Wed mornings.
- **Don't** automate. The hours you'd save in automation are the hours that
  destroy the open rate. Send by hand or use a tool that sends from your
  inbox one-by-one.

## Tracking

Sheet columns:

| Lawyer | Firm | Signal | Email 1 sent | Email 1 opened | Email 2 sent | Reply | Result |

Result codes: `D` demo booked / `T` started trial / `R` paid / `NF` no fit /
`S` not now (snooze 90d) / `B` bounced.

Goal: **15% reply rate by Day 21 → 8 demos → 3 paid customers**. That's a
3% conversion from cold email, which is excellent for a B2B-SaaS in legal.
