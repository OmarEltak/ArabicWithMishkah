## First-hour engagement plan

Within the first 60 minutes of posting, **every comment gets a reply**.
The algorithm's reach decision happens in this window.

Templates:

| Comment shape | Reply |
|---|---|
| "Congrats! Looks great" | "Thanks [Name]! If you're drafting under EG law this week, I'd love your feedback — DM me a sample contract you'd test it with and I'll run it through personally." |
| "How is this different from Harvey?" | "Great question — Harvey is brilliant for US/UK firms. We're built for the Arabic Civil Code, Cassation, and the bilingual export step that nobody else handles. Imagine Harvey if it grew up reading القانون المصري." |
| "Does it know Saudi law?" | "Yes — 11 MENA jurisdictions including Saudi (the Civil Transactions Law, Companies Law, Labor System). Egypt is what we've gone deepest on first." |
| "أين البيانات محفوظة" | "البيانات مشفّرة وعلى خوادم خارج الإمارات/السعودية للالتزام بقانون حماية البيانات الشخصية ١٥١/٢٠٢٠. مفيش تدريب على بياناتك." |
| "Pricing?" | "Solo at $50 (≈ EGP 999) / month, Firm at $150. Free 14-day trial, no card. The Solo plan recoups its cost on one contract that would have cost EGP 1,200 in outsourced translation." |
| Detractor / "AI can't replace lawyers" | "Agreed completely — it's a draft accelerator, not a lawyer-replacement. Every output cites the primary source so you can verify in 30 seconds before signing. The lawyer is still the lawyer." |
| DM request | Within 2 hours: send the founder's personal link to /eg, a screenshot, and 2-line "what would you draft with this first?" question to start a real conversation. |

**Do NOT** auto-like comments. Reply by hand. The persona-fit lawyer can spot a
template from kilometres away — and a real reply from a founder is worth a
hundred likes from a marketing bot.
