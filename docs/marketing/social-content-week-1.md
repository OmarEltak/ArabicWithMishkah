## Editorial guardrails (apply to every post)

1. **Always show, don't claim.** "Drafted in 22 min" with a screenshot beats
   "Saves you time" without one.
2. **Name a real person when you can.** "Mostafa, partner at..." humanises.
3. **No buzzwords.** "Leverages AI", "next-generation", "transformative" —
   never. The Cairo lawyer persona can spot marketing-speak in two words.
4. **Arabic posts get Arabic engagement, English posts get English.** Don't
   bilingual-stack — choose the language for the persona.
5. **One CTA per post.** Either /eg or a reply prompt. Never both.
6. **Always reply within 60 minutes.** The algo decides reach in this window.
7. **The founder writes every post.** Branded posts that sound like
   founder posts move 5× the engagement of branded posts that sound like a
   social-media manager.
8. **Numbers > adjectives.** "312 signups" not "lots of interest".
9. **Mistakes are content.** "Killed the paid campaign on day 4" is the
   most-respected sentence in the Sun post above. Show your work.
10. **Never apologise for the pricing.** EGP 999 is a confident number. Say
    it, then anchor it. Don't hedge.
