Source-of-truth list for marketing-lead + founder outreach during launch month.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't** sponsor a generic law conference (e.g. Cairo Business Law conference). The audience is too senior, too in-house counsel-heavy at large multinationals, and Egypt's most senior lawyers are NOT the wedge — boutique partners are.
- **Don't** advertise at university job fairs. Law-student attention burns ad spend with zero conversion potential for two years.
- **Don't** pay for a Cairo Bar Association directory ad. The directory is read by jobseekers, not buyers.
- **Don't** post product launch news in the "Egyptian Streets" classifieds. Wrong audience.
- **Don't** join 8 Facebook groups and post the same launch link in all of them. Egypt's professional lawyer community is small — coordinated multi-post = brand-damage flag in 24 hours.

## Founder time allocation (recommended)

| Activity | Hours/week (launch month) | After launch month |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn organic | 5 | 3 |
| WhatsApp 1:1 + broadcasts | 6 | 3 |
| Bar Association / event presence | 4 | 2 |
| Founder customer calls | 4 | 6 (more, as paid customers grow) |
| Community engagement (Facebook/Telegram/Reddit) | 2 | 1 |
| Press / podcast pitches | 2 | 1 |
| **Total** | **23 hrs/wk** | **16 hrs/wk** |

The other half of the founder's time goes to product. **Maintain this split
or growth will plateau by month 3** — every successful B2B founder who hits
a wall at month 3 cut growth time the moment the first paid customers came in.
