Free 14-day trial, no card. Would love your feedback — especially if you
work in or with the region.

## First-hour outreach plan (the launch-day hustle)

PH ranking is decided in the first 4 hours. Your goal: maximise comments
and upvotes from real users (not "first!" comments — substantive ones).

**T-30 min (9:30am Cairo):**
- Founder posts a "we just launched on Product Hunt" LinkedIn post + Twitter
- Brand page reposts the LinkedIn 5 min later
- WhatsApp broadcast to your warm list (template below)

**T+0 (10:01am Cairo, the moment PH refreshes):**
- Post the maker comment immediately (don't wait)
- Three pre-warned friends/customers post their own substantive comments
  (NOT "great product!" — real "I used this last week to draft X" stories)

**T+0 to T+4hr — every 20 minutes:**
- Reply to every PH comment (this is critical for PH ranking)
- DM 5 people from your network with: "Hi [Name], we just launched today
  on PH — if it's any use to you would love your honest feedback. Comment
  helps the algo more than the upvote: [link]"
- Refresh /eg in another tab — every 100 PH-driven visits should produce
  ~10 trial sign-ups; track in real time

**T+4hr to T+24hr:**
- Twitter post: "Day's not over but PH is going well — here's what we've
  learned from the comments so far" (with screenshots)
- Reply rate stays > 80%

**T+24hr:**
- Founder posts results: "we finished day N (#whatever) on PH". Numbers
  build narrative for the brand.

## Pre-warmed friends (the "hunters")

Ask 3–5 people **two days before launch** to:
- Be online at 10:00am Cairo on launch day
- Post a substantive comment within the first 30 min
- Not coordinate language with each other (PH detects identical phrasing)

Sample of comments you should ask for (NEVER write them yourself — describe
the shape and let the person tell their own story):

- A customer who drafted a real contract: "I drafted a shareholders
  agreement in 20 min that would've taken me 3 hours. The counter-proposal
  diff feature alone is worth it."
- A peer founder: "Watched Omar build this for 6 months. The depth on
  Egyptian civil law is genuinely unique."
- A lawyer outside Egypt: "I'm in [Riyadh/Dubai], the Saudi corpus is
  already deeper than I expected. Curious to see this grow."

---

## What to do if it goes flat

If by T+2 hours the rank is > #10 with no clear momentum, **stop fighting
the algo and start using the traffic you do have:**

- Post a "we're on PH, regardless of where we finish, here's what we
  learned from the comments today" LinkedIn post with screenshots
- The PH listing stays live forever — every backlink from PH to /eg is a
  long-term SEO win
- DM every PH commenter with a personal thank-you within 24 hrs

PH is a launch *channel*, not a launch *event*. The page is permanent and
keeps producing trickle traffic for years.
