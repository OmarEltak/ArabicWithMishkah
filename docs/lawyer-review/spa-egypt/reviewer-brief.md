# Reviewer Brief — Egyptian SPA Template

Thank you for taking the time to redline these drafts. The goal is to make
the My-lawyer SPA template ready to use on real deals under Egyptian law.

## Scope

You will see 5 SPA drafts, each generated by My-lawyer for a different deal
shape (mid-market acquisition, founder buyout, PE secondary, LLC quota
transfer, staged acquisition). They are produced from the same underlying
template; the variations show how the template behaves with different facts.

We are NOT asking you to draft from scratch. We are asking: **where is this
template wrong, missing, or sloppy under Egyptian law?**

## What to check

### 1. Citations (highest priority)

For every statute reference (Article X of Law Y) in the draft:

- **Is the article number correct?** My-lawyer claims to verify these against
  the actual code text, but it may be wrong in places.
- **Is this the right law to cite?** For an SPA we expect citations to:
  - Egyptian Civil Code (Law 131/1948), Articles 418, 429 (sale obligations),
    147–148 (pacta sunt servanda, good faith)
  - Egyptian Companies Law (Law 159/1981), Articles 85, 88, 291 (LLC quotas
    transferability, pre-emptive rights)
  - Capital Market Law (Law 95/1992), Article 353 (mandatory tender offer)
    where the target is a listed entity
- **Is the citation form idiomatic?** Egyptian lawyers cite as "المادة 418
  من القانون المدني المصري" — flag any non-idiomatic forms.

`source-articles.md` lists what My-lawyer believes the controlling articles
are. Mark anything in that list that is wrong or missing.

### 2. Clause structure & numbering

- Are the clauses in the order Egyptian counsel expects?
- Are any required clauses missing?
- Are there clauses that don't belong in an Egyptian SPA (e.g. boilerplate
  imported from US/UK precedent)?

Common Egyptian SPA structure:
1. Definitions
2. Sale and Purchase
3. Purchase Price and Adjustments
4. Conditions Precedent
5. Closing Mechanics
6. Representations and Warranties (Seller / Buyer / Target)
7. Indemnification + Limitation
8. Restrictive Covenants
9. Termination
10. Confidentiality
11. Notices
12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution (Cairo Economic Courts default)

### 3. Phrasing

- Does the Arabic read like Egyptian legal Arabic, or is it stiff/awkward?
- Are technical terms used in the civil-law sense (good faith ≠ common-law
  good faith, force majeure ≠ frustration, etc.)?
- Any English-influenced calques that should be rewritten?

### 4. Practical Egyptian-specific issues

- **Stamping** — the SPA needs to be stamped at GAFI / the Tax Authority.
  Does the draft acknowledge this?
- **Notarization** — any required ratification clauses?
- **GAFI registration** — for share transfers in JSCs, is the GAFI notification
  flagged?
- **FX restrictions** — purchase price denominated in foreign currency: any
  Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) notification or licence flag needed?
- **Capital Market Authority (FRA)** — if target is listed, mandatory tender
  offer / disclosure flags?

### 5. Red-team the AI

If you spot a citation that is **plausible but wrong**, that's the most valuable
finding — it tells us where the model is hallucinating. Mark these clearly
with `❌ HALLUCINATED` so we can fix the retrieval logic.

## How to redline

- Use track-changes in Word, or annotate the markdown with `[redline: ...]`
  comments — whichever is faster for you.
- If a clause needs to be replaced wholesale, write the replacement clause
  inline.
- If a clause needs to be deleted, strike it through.
- If a clause is missing, write it where it belongs and label `[add: NEW]`.

## When you're done

Fill in `intake-form.md` with:
- Your name, bar number, date
- A short summary of the most important changes
- Per-draft severity flags ("acceptable as-is", "needs minor fixes",
  "needs significant rework", "do not use")
- Whether you're willing to be credited by name on our homepage
  (we'd love to credit you, but it's optional)

Send everything back as a zip or via the same channel this packet arrived in.
