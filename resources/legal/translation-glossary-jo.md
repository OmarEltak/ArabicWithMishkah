# Jordanian Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under the law of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan.
Jordan's civil-law tradition is based on the Mejelle (Ottoman) and the
Egyptian Sanhouri model, with Islamic-law roots stronger than in some
Gulf jurisdictions. Legislation is issued as "قانون رقم N لسنة YYYY".

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`
