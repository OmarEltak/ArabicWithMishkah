# Kuwaiti Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under Kuwaiti law. Kuwait's legal tradition descends from
the same Sanhouri civil-law base as Egypt; key statutes are issued as
"قانون رقم N لسنة YYYY" — preserve the citation form.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`
