# Bahraini Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under the law of the Kingdom of Bahrain. Bahrain's
private-law tradition descends from the Egyptian Sanhouri civil-law model;
key legislation is issued as "قانون رقم N لسنة YYYY" or "مرسوم بقانون
رقم N لسنة YYYY" — preserve the citation form.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`
