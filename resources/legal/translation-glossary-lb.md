# Lebanese Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under Lebanese law. Lebanese law derives directly from
the French civil-law tradition (not the Sanhouri Egyptian-Arab tradition),
so the terminology cluster is distinct: قانون الموجبات والعقود instead
of قانون مدني, "موجب" alongside "التزام", "إقتضاء" instead of "إعذار".
Translate to French-civilian English, not Egyptian-civilian English.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`
