# Saudi Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under Saudi Arabian law (KSA). Saudi law uses نظام (system /
regulation) rather than قانون (law) for primary legislation; royal decrees
follow the form م/N — these conventions must be preserved.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`

## Core legislative form

نظام | regulation / law | (Saudi primary legislation; "law" is acceptable for non-specialist English)
المرسوم الملكي | Royal Decree
المرسوم الملكي رقم م/N | Royal Decree No. M/N
لائحة | regulation (executive)
اللائحة التنفيذية | Implementing Regulation
الأمر الملكي | Royal Order
قرار مجلس الوزراء | Council of Ministers' Resolution
