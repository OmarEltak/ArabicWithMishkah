# Omani Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under the law of the Sultanate of Oman. Omani legislation
is issued as "مرسوم سلطاني رقم N/YYYY" (Royal Decree). Preserve the
citation form. Oman's civil tradition is an outgrowth of the Egyptian
Sanhouri model with adjustments.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`
