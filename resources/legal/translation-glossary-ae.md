# UAE Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under United Arab Emirates law. UAE primary legislation is
issued as قانون اتحادي (federal law) or مرسوم بقانون اتحادي (federal decree-
law); emirate-specific laws follow their own format. Both must be preserved
exactly.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`

## Core legislative form

قانون اتحادي | Federal Law
المرسوم بقانون اتحادي | Federal Decree-Law
قانون | Law
مرسوم | Decree
قرار مجلس الوزراء | Cabinet Resolution
القرار الوزاري | Ministerial Resolution
