# Qatari Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

Authoritative terminology lock for English working translations of Arabic
contracts drafted under Qatari law. Qatar's primary legislation is issued
as "قانون رقم N لسنة YYYY" — preserve the citation form. The QFC
(Qatar Financial Centre) operates a parallel English common-law system;
this glossary covers the Arabic-language onshore Qatari law.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note`
