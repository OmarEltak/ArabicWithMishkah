# Egyptian Civil-Law → English Legal-Translation Glossary

This is the **authoritative terminology lock** for any English working translation
of an Arabic-language contract drafted under Egyptian law. The translator MUST
use the listed English equivalent when the Arabic term appears, because these
are terms of art whose civil-law meaning differs from the literal English.

Format: `Arabic | English | Note (when ambiguous)`
