Press **⌘K** (macOS) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) on any authenticated page to open the global search palette.

## What you can find

- **Contracts** by title
- **Matters** by client or matter name
- **Templates** by name or category
- **Clippings** (saved research excerpts)
- **Legal documents** (laws + cassation principles) by title

…plus quick-action shortcuts to every section of the app (new draft, new matter, audit log, etc.).

## Keyboard

- **↑ / ↓** to navigate results
- **↵ Enter** to open the highlighted result
- **Esc** to close

The palette searches everything you own (per-user scope) plus the public legal corpus. There's no cross-tenant exposure — you only see your contracts, your matters, your clippings.
