A **matter** is a folder for everything connected to one piece of work: contracts, chat sessions, sometimes a single client name across many engagements.

## Create a matter

Go to **Operations → Matters → New matter**. Add:

- **Client name** (e.g. "Acme Inc.")
- **Matter name** (e.g. "Q2 vendor onboarding")
- **Internal reference** (optional, your own tracking ID)
- **Notes** (scope, key contacts, deadlines)

Status starts as **Active**. Archive it when the engagement closes — archived matters disappear from filters but stay searchable.

## Assign a contract to a matter

Two ways:

1. **Inline** on the contract editor — there's a matter dropdown at the top of every contract. Switching it logs an audit entry.
2. **Bulk** — on the contracts list, click **Select**, tick multiple rows, then use the **Move to matter** dropdown in the toolbar.

## Filter

The contracts sidebar has an "All matters / Unassigned / specific matter" dropdown. URL-friendly: `/lawyer/contracts?matter=5` deep-links to all contracts for matter 5.

## Why this matters

Once you have more than ~20 contracts, the flat list becomes unmanageable. Matters give you a working hierarchy without a heavyweight project-management overlay.
