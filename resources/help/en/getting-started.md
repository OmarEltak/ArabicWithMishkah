My-lawyer drafts citation-grounded bilingual contracts under MENA jurisdictions in three steps.

## 1. Describe what you need

Open **Drafting → New draft**. Write a sentence or two with the parties, jurisdiction, and key terms. Arabic and English both work. Example:

> An NDA between ACME Inc. and Globex Corp. for a 2-year mutual disclosure under Egyptian law. Confidential information includes business plans, customer data, and source code.

If you've created templates, pick one to seed the draft with standard sections — otherwise leave blank.

## 2. Answer clarifying questions

The assistant asks up to three rounds of short questions before it drafts: things like effective date, exact party names, governing-law specifics. Each round is a single message — answer briefly, the AI fills in the rest.

You can skip clarification entirely by clicking **Draft now** at any point.

## 3. Review the corpus gate

Every clause is checked against the live legal corpus. The draft lands on the **Contracts** page with:

- A **citation panel** listing every "Article N of Law Y" reference and whether the corpus could verify it
- A **needs-review banner** if any citation is unverified — fix or remove those before finalising

Once everything is verified, click **Finalize** and download the .pdf or .docx (single-language or side-by-side bilingual).
