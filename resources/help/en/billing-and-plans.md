## Plans

| Plan | Drafts / month | Other |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 3 | All 11 MENA jurisdictions, bilingual export (watermarked) |
| **Solo** | 25 | No watermark, full revision history, email support |
| **Firm** | 200 | Bulk export, team-priority queue |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited | Contact us |

The "drafts/month" counter is per calendar month, resets on the 1st. **Edits to an existing draft don't count** — only generating a fresh contract from a new chat session counts.

## Subscribing

From the **Pricing** page or **Settings → Billing**, click **Start Solo trial** (or Firm). You'll be redirected to Stripe Checkout. After payment, the plan is active immediately; a 14-day free trial means no card is charged for the first two weeks.

## Managing your subscription

**Settings → Billing → Manage billing** opens the Stripe Customer Portal. From there you can:

- Change plan (monthly ↔ yearly, Solo ↔ Firm)
- Update payment method
- Download past invoices
- Cancel — the plan stays active until the end of the paid period, then drops to Free.

## Usage tracking

**Operations → Usage & cost** shows your daily AI-call spend (separately from the plan-level draft cap). Both your personal daily cap and the global cap are enforced per-call, so a runaway loop can't drain your budget.
